Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Tantrum Games Studio ("we," "us," or "our") operates mobile and PC applications and games (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our games.

By using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store directly identifiable personal information (such as your real name, email address, or phone number) unless you explicitly provide it to us. However, our games automatically utilize third-party services that collect device-specific data to function properly.

Automatically Collected Data (Via Third-Party SDKs):

  • Identifiers: Device IP addresses, Android Advertising ID (GAID), and unique mobile installation identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Game performance metrics, crash logs, multiplayer match connection states, and leaderboard scores.

2. How Your Data Is Used

We use the information collected to:

  • Facilitate real-time, peer-to-peer, and client-server multiplayer networking.
  • Maintain and display global, in-game competitive leaderboards.
  • Serve, optimize, and personalize in-game advertisements.
  • Monitor game stability, fix bugs, and prevent exploit behavior.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver our game experiences, we partner with reliable third-party networks. These services process your data independently under their own privacy frameworks. You can review their policies at the links below:

  • Photon Fusion 2 (Exit Games): Handles real-time multiplayer networking synchronization and room matchmaking.
    Exit Games Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Serves non-personalized or personalized banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements.
    Google Privacy & Terms
  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Handles real-time server database storage for in-game leaderboards, high scores, and app quality diagnostics.
    Firebase Privacy & Security Terms

4. Cookies and Device Identifiers

Our third-party advertising partners use mobile device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to track ad delivery performance and prevent fraud. You can reset or opt out of these tracking identifiers at any time via your Android device's system settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's Privacy

Our games are not intentionally directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete it from our systems. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided data, please contact us.

6. International Data Transfers

Your information, including data processed by Firebase and Photon, may be transferred to—and maintained on—computers located outside of your state, province, or country, where data protection laws may differ.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
